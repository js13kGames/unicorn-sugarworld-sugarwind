**Athena has one wing. She uses it as a sail.** Ride the sugar dunes at the speed
that keeps you ahead of what is behind you.

### Controls

- **Touch:** swipe <kbd>→</kbd> to catch a gust, swipe <kbd>←</kbd> to spill the wind
- **Keyboard:** <kbd>D</kbd> to gust, <kbd>A</kbd> to slow

That is the whole scheme. Everything else is about *when*.

### How it plays

Speed is what you manage, not obstacles. Hit a crest fast and you launch, which is
where you are safe and where you cover ground. Hit one slow and you bury the nose.
Syrup pools grab you if you enter them fast. Anywhere too slow, the thing behind
you closes.

Each level is one minute. Reach the end and it is yours. Progress saves, so you can
close the tab and come back.

### Story

Athena's people can all fly. She cannot, so she learned to sail instead, and this
trip goes further than she meant it to.

Second in *Unicorn Sugarworld*, after **Rainbow Tiles**, leading into **Looking
Up**. Each stands alone.

Every colour is drawn by a formula, every sound synthesized in the browser, nothing
stored. Runs on a phone.
